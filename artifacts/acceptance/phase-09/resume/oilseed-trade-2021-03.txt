PROJECTION FOR 2020/21
Global 2020/21 oilseed production is forecast at 596 million tons, 750,000 tons above
February. Higher Brazil soybean production coupled with greater Australia and European
Union rapeseed production more than offsets lower palm kernel, cottonseed, and
sunflowerseed production forecasts.
Global oilseed exports, at 195 million tons, are up 820,000 tons primarily on higher Australia
and Ukraine rapeseed exports. Oilseed crush is raised 1,210,000 tons to 514 million. Larger
Brazil and Argentina soybean crush and European Union rapeseed crush more than offset
lower China soybean crush. Global oilseed stocks are up 760,000 tons mostly on higher Brazil
and China soybean stocks more than offsetting lower Argentina soybean stocks.
Protein meal production is up 1,150,000 tons and ending stocks are up 910,000 tons. Protein
meal exports are raised 890,000 tons due to a 1-million-ton increase in Argentina soybean
meal shipments partially offset by other changes. Global vegetable oil production is down
200,000 tons despite higher crush as lower palm oil production more than offsets higher
rapeseed oil and soybean oil production. Vegetable oil exports are up 350,000 tons on higher
Argentina soy oil and Black Sea sunflower oil. Ending stocks are up 520,000 tons primarily
due to higher India palm oil carryout. The projected U.S. season-average farm price for
soybeans is unchanged at $11.15 per bushel.

Export prices for the three largest soybean exporters were given a slight reprieve as average prices
declined in February on expectations of seasonally large new-crop exportable supplies in Brazil and
Argentina. Prices in the United States and Argentina remained relatively flat while Brazil fell slightly as
expectations of a large crop were mostly offset by slow harvest progress.
