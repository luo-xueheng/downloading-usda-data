OVERVIEW 2026/27

Global oilseed production is                         lowe red this month on reduced China peanuts, European Union
rapeseed, and India soybeans, more than offsetting                               higher U.S. soybeans. Global oilseed trade is
raised on higher U.S. soybean exports.                                              Global crush is also unchanged as higher                   Argentina soybean
and Australia rapeseed offset lower                     Indonesia palm kernel crushing. Global oilseed carryout                  is
reduced on European Union and Australia rapeseed                                                         and Argentina soybean stocks                           that more than
offset                   increased Brazil and India soybean and Russia sunflowerseed  stocks.                           Global protein meal
trade is raised as higher Argentina soybean meal exports more than offset lower Indonesia palm
kernel meal exports. Global vegetable oil trade is lowered          on reduced Indonesia palm oil exports,
more than offsetting higher Malaysia and Guatemala palm oil and Argentina soybean oil exports.
Global vegetable oil carryout is lowe red                                        on reduced Indonesia and India palm oil .

The projected U.S. season-average farm price for  soybeans is                          unchanged at $12.00 per bushel.

EXPORT PRICES
U.S. soybean prices were largely range            bound in the 3 weeks preceding this WASDE  report.                                    A drop
in prices following the U.S.-China presidential  summit in Washington,                               D.C. was muted by a slightly
supportive NASS stocks report at the end of September and rainfall delaying harvest in the western
corn belt.                   U.S. soybean meal prices spiked to a  2-year high of $455/ton in late September on
dwindling soybean supplies available to crushers in some regions                   . Global meal prices have since
fallen from that level as harvest progress  in the United States            alleviates tightness in U.S.                                          crushing
supplies.

U.S. soybean oil prices fell         during the second half of              September, broadly tracking declines in
petroleum prices. Palm oil prices           fell sharply, with both Indonesia and Mal aysia palm oil trading
below South American soybean oil. Abundant stocks and                         strong monthly production totals
reported by MPOB in Malaysia  prompted this slide, in addition to falling petroleum prices.

OTHER NOTES

Marketing Years (MY):  MY refers to the 12-month period at the onset of the main harvest, when the
crop is marketed (i.e., consumed, traded, or stored).   The year first listed begins a country's MY for
that commodity (2022/23 starts in 2022); except for oilseeds           in certain Southern Hemisphere
countries, where the second year begins the MY (2022/23 starts in 2023). For a complete list of
local marketing years, please see the FAS website (https://apps.fas.usda.gov/psdonline/): go to
Reports, Reference Data, and then Data Availability.

Stocks: Unless otherwise stated, stock data are based on an aggregate of differing local marketing
years and should not be  construed as representing world stock levels at a fixed point in time.

Consumption: World totals for consumption reflect total utilization, including food, seed,
industrial, feed, and waste; as well as differences in local marketing year imports and local
marketing year exports.  Consumption statistics for regions and individual countries, however,
reflect food, seed, industrial, feed, and waste only.

Trade: All PSD tables are balanced on the different local marketing years.
