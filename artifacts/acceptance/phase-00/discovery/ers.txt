Commodity Costs and Returns
Interactive Visualization: U.S. Commodity Costs and Returns by Region and by Commodity
Organic Costs and Returns
Readings
Documentation
Cost and return estimates are reported for the United States and major production regions for corn, soybeans, wheat, cotton, grain sorghum, rice, peanuts, oats, barley, milk, hogs, and cow-calf. The series of commodity cost and return estimates for the
U.S. and regions
is divided into
Recent
and
Historical
estimates. Recent estimates date back to the most recent major revision in accounting methods, account format, and regional definitions for each commodity. Historical estimates date back to when the series began.
Cost-of-Production Forecasts
are also available for major U.S. field crops.
Organic Costs and Returns
for corn, milk, wheat, and soybeans are also available.
Also, see the ERS
Milk Cost of Production Estimates data product
.
